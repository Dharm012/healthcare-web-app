HealthConnect: Smart Telehealth & Medicine Prescription System

Solving Telemedicine Gaps with Real-Time Video Prescribing, Auto-Schedules & Daily Reminders

Executive Summary: HealthConnect is a modern digital health platform that combines encrypted HD video consultations with an in-call medicine prescription builder and an automated daily reminder system. It ensures patients never miss their medicine doses while allowing doctors to issue digitally signed prescriptions in seconds.

1. The Problem Statement (What Was Wrong Before?)

Online doctor consultations have grown rapidly, but traditional telemedicine tools suffer from critical disconnects between the live video call, prescription writing, and actual patient recovery:

1. Disconnected Prescriptions

Doctors usually had to leave the video call or use separate paper notes to write prescriptions. This wastes time, causes errors, and delays treatment.

2. High Medication Non-Adherence

Over 50% of patients forget their dosage timings or stop taking medications early once symptoms fade, causing health relapses and hospital readmissions.

3. Zero Tracking for Doctors

Doctors had no visibility into whether patients took their medicines on time, skipped doses, or experienced side effects after the call ended.

4. Technical Video & Chat Glitches

Common video consultation tools suffer from black video screens during connection, dropped candidates, and duplicate chat message bugs that confuse patients.

2. Our Solution (What We Built to Solve These Problems)

We built an integrated end-to-end medical solution directly into the video consultation room and portal dashboards:

💊 In-Call Live Medicine Prescribing

Doctors prescribe medicines directly inside the video room without leaving the patient. Includes quick autocomplete, multi-time chips (Morning, Afternoon, Night), duration counters, and continuous autosave.

📅 Automated Reminder Schedule Engine

When the call finishes, our background engine automatically calculates Start Date + Duration and creates daily scheduled dose entries in the database for every single time slot across all days.

✅ Interactive Patient Adherence Tracker

Patients see today's pending medicines on their dashboard with exact timings, food instructions, and day progress ("Day 1 of 5"). They can click "Mark as Taken" or "Skip with Reason" with 1 tap.

🔒 Ultra-Reliable HD Video & Single-Instance Chat

Fixed WebRTC ICE candidate dropping for instant video feeds with zero black screens, plus message deduplication so in-call chats and shared prescriptions never appear twice.

3. How It Works (Step-by-Step Patient & Doctor Journey)

Step 1
Secure Video Consultation: Doctor and patient join the encrypted video room with synchronized audio/video and in-call messaging.
Step 2
Real-Time Medicine Drafting: Doctor switches to 'Medicines' tab, types diagnosis, selects medications, picks daily time chips (e.g., 08:00 AM & 08:00 PM), and sets 5 days duration. Draft autosaves instantly.
Step 3
Review & Finalize Rx: Doctor clicks 'Finalize Prescription'. The prescription locks, and can be shared directly into the chat for live patient confirmation.
Step 4
Automatic Schedule Generation: When consultation ends, our backend automatically generates individual daily dose records in the database for all 5 days.
Step 5
Daily Reminders & Compliance: Patient logs into dashboard, sees today's doses, marks 'Taken' (logs timestamp) or 'Skipped' (records reason), and prints official Rx.

4. Why It Is Beneficial (Impact & Real-World Value)

👤 Benefits for Patients

  • Never Miss a Dose: Clear daily schedule shows exactly what pill to take at what time.
  • Zero Confusion: Includes clear meal instructions (e.g. "Take after food with warm water").
  • 100% Digital & Paperless: Access official printable prescriptions anytime from any device.
  • Better Health Outcomes: High medication adherence ensures fast and complete recovery.

👨‍⚕️ Benefits for Doctors

  • Speed & Efficiency: Create complete prescriptions in under 30 seconds inside video call.
  • Safety Safeguards: Unfinished draft modal alerts doctor before closing call accidentally.
  • Patient Adherence History: View full logs of taken and skipped doses during follow-ups.
  • Standardized Digital Records: Auto-formatted with MCI registration license & hospital headers.
